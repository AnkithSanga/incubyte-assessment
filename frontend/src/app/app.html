Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Scope

1. Objective

Transition ACME Organization from fragmented, error-prone Excel spreadsheets to a high-performance web-based Salary Management and Compensation Intelligence platform for 10,000+ international employees.

2. In-Scope MVP Features

  • Salary Management: Full CRUD operations (Add, View, Edit, Delete) across 10,000 employees with instant pagination and multi-dimensional filtering.
  • Multi-Country Normalization: Store native local currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, CAD) with automatic reference FX conversion into USD for organizational reporting.
  • Visual Intelligence: Executive KPI cards, Department breakdown chart, Geographic payroll share, and Salary band distribution histogram.
  • Deterministic Seeding: Sub-second automated seeding of 10,000 realistic international records via JPA batching.
  • Compensation Assistant: Natural language query assistant for HR leadership inquiries.

3. Deliberate Scope Exclusions (With Rationale)

  • In-App File Upload: Confirmed out of scope by stakeholder; automated 10k database seeder fulfills evaluation needs cleanly.
  • Bonuses, Equity & Taxes: Focused strictly on Annual Base Salary to evaluate core salary craftsmanship without statutory tax engine bloat.
  • Auth & RBAC: Assumes single pre-authenticated internal HR Manager persona to focus on salary management and analytics.
